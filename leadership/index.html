Ambassador Idd Bedel Mohamed

Chairman

Ambassador Idd Bedel Mohamed is an accomplished statesman, distinguished diplomat, and strategic leader with decades of experience in international relations, national security, and senior government leadership. Throughout his distinguished career, he has demonstrated a steadfast commitment to representing Somalia on the global stage, strengthening international partnerships, and advancing the nation’s interests through strategic diplomacy and public service.

Abdirizak Mohamed Mohamud

Vice Chair

A distinguished political leader, academic and consultant with experience in public administration, institutional leadership and private-sector development. He notably served as Minister of Transport and Aviation of Galmudug State, and has advised the World Bank, the Federal Government of Somalia's Ministry of Transport and Aviation, and private-sector institutions on transport, trade and investment.
